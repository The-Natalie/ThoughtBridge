ThoughtBridge

An AI-powered memory and learning app for people who think differently.

ThoughtBridge helps people retain what they learn, recognize what they’ve accomplished, and communicate what they know in interviews, meetings, and work.

Built with neurodivergent thinkers in mind, ThoughtBridge addresses a common challenge: understanding complex systems and solving real problems, but struggling to recall terminology, recognize accomplishments, or explain knowledge on demand.

Core Features

ThoughtBridge is designed around three connected capabilities:

🧠 Personalized Learning

* Learn technical concepts through familiar-system analogies and an interactive eight-step explanation process.
* Ask questions, explore confusion, and resume explanations.
* Save explanations and mark what made a concept click.
* Practice explaining concepts in your own words with AI-generated feedback.

📝 Work Documentation & Accomplishment Recognition

* Capture work notes, development sessions, problems solved, and decisions made.
* Identify demonstrated skills and accomplishments that might otherwise go unrecognized.
* Preserve reasoning, tradeoffs, rejected approaches, and outcomes.
* Connect extracted information to its original sources.

💬 Interview Preparation

* Practice technical and experience-based interview questions.
* Ground experience-based answers in documented work.
* Receive actionable feedback on accuracy, missing details, and terminology.
* Revisit previous learning sessions, decisions, and conversations.

Technology Stack

* Backend: Python, FastAPI, Pydantic
* Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
* Database: SQLite, FTS5, sqlite-vec
* Retrieval: Hybrid keyword and semantic search (RAG)
* AI: Meta Model API
* Embeddings: fastembed

Project Philosophy

Understanding something and being able to explain it on demand are not the same skill.

ThoughtBridge is designed to bridge that gap, helping people make their knowledge, reasoning, and contributions visible.
